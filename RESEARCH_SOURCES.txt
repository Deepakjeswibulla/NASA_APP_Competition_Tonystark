UNRESOLVED CONTRADICTIONS BETWEEN SOURCES
==============================================================================

[surveyor-3] Sources give the Apollo 12 LM's distance from Surveyor 3 as 155 m (NASA Science feature) and 180 m (Surveyor 3 mission page); both are recorded, the closer figure is used in the narrative with the range shown.

[surveyor-3] Mass of returned material is quoted as 'about 10 kg' by the Smithsonian summary of the mission; the 1972 NASA report lists the individual items instead of a total.

[apollo-14-alsep] The deployment distance is given as about 600 ft (180 m) by NASA History; the seismic-station catalogue lists the seismometer 178 m from the lunar module. Both agree within a few metres.

[apollo-15-lrv] Some popular sources quote 27.9 km, others 28.1 km for the same traverse; the mission report and LROC both give 27.9 km, which is used here.

[apollo-16-fuv-camera] Mass is quoted as 22 kg in the instrument literature and as '50 pounds' in one NASA feature; these agree, and 22 kg is used.

[apollo-17-lrv] The 35.7 km figure comes from the rover odometer. A 2019 photogrammetric reconstruction of the traverses gives 31.2 km of actual ground path, about 2 km longer than the mapped profile in the same study — the odometer over-reads. Both values are recorded here rather than one being silently chosen.

[mars-pathfinder-lander] The landing coordinates are recorded with an explicit discrepancy between NASA, NSSDC and JPL/Photojournal values; the marker is therefore marked approximate.

[sojourner-rover] Total odometry is usually quoted as 'about 100 m'; a precise published odometer total was not found in the sources reviewed.

[spirit-rover] NASA Science gives the end of recovery efforts as 25 May 2011; the JPL end-of-mission report describes recovery activity concluding on 13 July 2011. Both are recorded.

[insight-lander] The heat-probe failure depth is reported as about 40 cm in the mission literature; the instrument's exact final depth is not quoted here.

[viking-1-lander] Two coordinate sets are published for this site (see map note); the discrepancy is recorded rather than resolved silently.
